Pan Yao

Santoo Institute for Structural Studies

About

Pan Yao is an artist and researcher based in the field of structural painting and cognitive structure studies. He is the founder of the Santoo Institute for Structural Studies.

His work focuses on the visualization of cognitive stability through structural configurations, exploring the interaction between perceptual fields, energy distribution, and decision nodes.

Field

Structural Painting / Cognitive Structure
